Who this master's is for

Built for a credible pivot into AI

  • Engineers and computer science graduates ready to specialise in AI and applied data science.
  • IT and software professionals expanding into machine learning, analytics and cloud AI systems.
  • Non-technical professionals seeking a structured, credible pivot into AI — Python and applied AI tools are taught from the ground up; prior coding experience is not required for admission.
  • Working professionals who want a postgraduate-level qualification (EQF Level 7), not a shortcut — plan for 4–6 hours a day alongside live weekend sessions.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply

Do I need a coding background to apply?

No. A bachelor's degree in any discipline is accepted for admission. Python programming and applied AI tools are taught within the curriculum — coding is part of what you'll learn, not a prerequisite to get in.

Is GEM Alpine Business School accredited?

GEM Alpine Business School holds the Triple Crown of accreditation — AMBA, AACSB and EQUIS — held by fewer than 1% of business schools worldwide, and its programmes are delivered under university accreditation standards. Accreditation applies to the school.

How long is the programme and how is it delivered?

12 months, 90 ECTS credits (EQF Level 7), delivered fully online with live weekly sessions plus independent LMS-based study, simulations and case labs.

Is work experience required?

It's preferred but not mandatory. If you hold a relevant bachelor's degree and are ready for the workload, you're welcome to apply.

How much time should I expect to commit each week?

Plan for roughly 4–6 hours a day alongside live Friday/Saturday sessions — this is a rigorous postgraduate-level programme built for working professionals, not a light commitment.

Is there a fee to apply?

No. There is no fee to submit your application or request the brochure.
