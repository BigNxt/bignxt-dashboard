Reading these projections responsibly

1
Small samples move a lot. Kuwait and Google Ads are each built on fewer than 6 historical payments; the new Qatar & UAE LinkedIn line has no baseline at all. Their September numbers are directional, not forecasts — review actuals weekly and be ready to shift the AED 6,000 combined “Test” pool (Google + Qatar&UAE LinkedIn) toward whichever is actually converting by week 2.
2
Payments lag spend, often by months. This pipeline routinely sees payments land 6–12 months after the lead was generated. September’s admissions will include leads from campaigns run well before September — don’t judge September’s own campaigns by September’s payment count alone.
3
Both GCC benched rates carry risk, but Oman’s is the softer one. Qatar & UAE’s AED 50 is grounded in a real recent week (Aug 25–31 actual: AED 43.3); if that holds, AED 15,000 buys more than 300 leads. Oman’s AED 50 has no recent data behind it at all — its Facebook campaign hasn’t delivered since Aug 21, so the number is a planning assumption, not a benched rate the way Qatar & UAE’s is. Treat Oman’s 200-lead estimate as contingent on the relaunch happening and performing near this rate; if it delivers worse, the shortfall needs covering from elsewhere in the plan, not assumed away. Track combined lead volume weekly against the 5,000 ceiling — trim Kuwait or Google first if either GCC market is running hotter than benched.
4
30-payment floor now has almost no margin. ~31 projected payments clears the 30 target by just 1 — down from a 4-payment cushion before this round’s Oman/Kuwait increase and Africa trim, since GCC’s higher-CPL/lower-ROI economics now carry a bigger share of the plan. In the downside case (Kuwait, the new Qatar & UAE LinkedIn test, and Google all land at zero), the plan hits exactly 30 on the Africa markets, Oman, and the Africa LinkedIn line alone — no room left below that. Watch payments weekly, not just leads; if Oman’s relaunch slips past mid-September, this floor is at real risk.
5
Qatar & UAE’s focused CAC (AED 3,442) blends two winning campaigns only. Real-world CAC when scaling ad budget on the same audience often drifts upward (auction saturation) — treat 4 projected payments there as an upper bound, not a floor.
